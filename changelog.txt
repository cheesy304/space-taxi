  Bugfixes:
    - The ship now waits until your pod has landed on its hub. Before, it left as soon as you reached orbit, so it looked like it flew off without you.

Version: 0.3.1
Date: 2026-11-20
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed the rocket not launching when the ship arrived: pausing the ship started the launch a second time, which switched the silo off again.
    - Silos left switched off by this bug are switched back on, and a booking stuck as "launched" puts you back in a rocket.
    - A passenger who reached the ship is now always noticed, even if the surface change was missed.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.3.0
Date: 2026-11-20
  Features:
    - Ships are now ranked by estimated arrival time instead of distance. Space Taxi reads each stop's wait conditions (fixed time, time-capped, or open-ended such as "wait for items") and learns how long every ship really stays at each stop and how long each flight takes.
    - Destination bookings are no longer tied to one ship: until your rocket launches, Space Taxi switches to a ship that gets you there clearly sooner, or to a passenger ship that is in orbit right now and is no slower.
    - The travel window and the status line show the estimated time. A "?" marks a guess, when the route has an open-ended stop that has not been observed yet.
  Changes:
    - A ship paused by Space Taxi while a passenger boards still counts as being in automatic mode.
